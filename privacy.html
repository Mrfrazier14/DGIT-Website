WEBSITE PRIVACY NOTICE

Your inquiry.
Your information.

This notice covers inquiries made through the DivineGuard IT Services LLC website. Updated October 28, 2026.

Information you provide

Our forms request contact information and details relevant to your inquiry. Depending on the form, this may include your name, organization, email, optional phone number, service interest, project location, timeline, budget, public project reference, website, capabilities, availability and message. Please provide only information needed for the conversation.

How inquiry information is used

Information submitted through these forms is used to review your request, respond, clarify requirements and coordinate potential customer, provider, training, media or partnership conversations. A form submission does not enroll you in a paid service.

Hosting, forms and email

This website is hosted on Netlify. Form submissions are processed and stored through Netlify Forms, and submission notifications are delivered through email. Inquiry information may therefore be present in the form dashboard and business email records. Hosting and email providers process technical information needed to operate their services.

See Netlify’s privacy policy for its practices. The website’s external links lead to services with their own privacy practices.

Sensitive information

Do not submit passwords, API keys, access codes, payment card details, Social Security numbers, medical records, confidential personnel files or other sensitive records. If a project needs protected information or authorized system access, contact DivineGuard to agree on an appropriate process before sharing it.

Records and privacy requests

To ask about information you submitted, request a correction or request deletion, email AaronFrazier@DGITServices.onmicrosoft.com. Identify the form and approximate submission date; do not attach sensitive identity documents. We may need to confirm the request relates to your information and discuss any records that need to be retained for an active engagement or applicable obligations.

Changes and service-specific arrangements

We will update this page as the website’s inquiry practices change. Customer projects, approved third-party tools and AI workflows may require additional data-handling terms agreed for that engagement.
