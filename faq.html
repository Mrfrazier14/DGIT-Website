Questions before
getting started?

Understand the process, prepare your inquiry and find the right service.

Can you help remotely or on site?

DivineGuard is based in North Las Vegas, Nevada. Remote and on-site delivery, travel, site access and coverage are confirmed for each project. Nationwide inquiries are welcome; staffing and partner availability are assessed before commitment.

How do I request a quote?

Use the project inquiry form. Tell us your goal, current tools, team size, location and timeline. We review the request and clarify scope, availability and the next conversation.

What should I prepare?

Prepare a short description of the problem, desired outcome, relevant systems and your target date. A budget range is helpful if known. For government work, include the public notice or solicitation reference and deadline with its time zone.

When can work begin?

Scheduling depends on scope, access, staffing and any specialist requirements. A start date and delivery milestones are agreed in writing. Submitting an inquiry does not reserve a service window.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Project and ongoing support arrangements can be discussed. Coverage hours, response windows, included tasks, escalation and fees are set in the proposal. Contact us to confirm availability for time-sensitive requests.

What does program and logistics coordination cover?

Scheduling, resource coordination, milestone tracking, documentation and team communication. Explore Marcetta’s coordination services; delivery responsibilities and any transportation or specialist needs are scoped individually.

What does the $600/month Business AI service include?

One agreed AI-assisted business workflow, configuration, testing, guidance and a monthly review with minor refinements within scope. Read the service details. Support allowances, usage costs and billing terms must be specified in the written proposal before enrollment.

Are Gray Fox AI and GovCon Pulse available as finished subscriptions?

They are development initiatives and previews. Pilot readiness, availability and any customer delivery are confirmed individually. Visit Apps for the current descriptions.

How are specialist or government projects handled?

Specialist requirements are matched to qualified providers and an agreed delivery role. Each solicitation has its own eligibility and qualification requirements. Review the delivery classifications and government inquiry information.

Where do inquiry details go?

Website forms use Netlify Forms and inquiry notifications are delivered by email. Read the privacy notice for the information collected and how to request a correction or deletion. Do not send passwords, access codes, payment information or sensitive records.

How do payments, changes and cancellations work?

Payment arrangements, change approval, renewal and cancellation terms are defined in the written quote or service agreement. Ask for clarification before accepting an engagement. The website inquiry form does not charge a payment or begin a subscription.
