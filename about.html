FOUNDER PROFILE

Aaron Frazier Jr.

Founder & Principal Engineer / AI Engineer

Aaron founded DivineGuard IT Services LLC to connect practical IT support with software development, security awareness and technology education. He leads the company’s direction and brings its service planning back to a straightforward question: what does the organization need to accomplish, and what is required to deliver it responsibly?

His work at DivineGuard spans support workflows, Microsoft 365 assistance, web and application projects, API integration planning, documentation and emerging AI workflows. Current product-development initiatives include GovCon Pulse opportunity tracking and AI workflow tools. Each project is assessed on its actual readiness before a production commitment is offered.

Faith and community

Aaron is a man of faith and has been part of his current church community for more than 22 years. His faith is a personal foundation for how he approaches his life, relationships and work.

Education and continued learning

Aaron holds associate’s and bachelor’s degrees in cybersecurity from Southern New Hampshire University (SNHU). He is a member of the UNLV Cyber Clinic and is continuing his training there, developing his cybersecurity practice through ongoing learning and participation.

Career background

In 2025, Aaron served as an IT Consultant at New Horizon Medical Solutions, supporting cybersecurity, IT operations and software development. His work supported the business under CEO Will’s leadership.

Aaron’s professional background includes experience with Amazon, Precisely and Switch. His career interests connect the day-to-day needs of IT users with the broader work of cybersecurity, software development and AI engineering. At DivineGuard, that combination informs how he approaches requirements, troubleshooting, system improvements and practical tools for organizations.

Leadership and academic recognition

He also serves as a committee chair with the National Society of Leadership and Success (NSLS). His academic recognition includes nine Honor Roll recognitions, two Dean’s List recognitions and one President’s List recognition.

Technical focus and skills

Aaron’s areas of work include IT consulting and troubleshooting, Microsoft 365 support, cybersecurity awareness and access hygiene, web and software development, frontend and backend workflows, APIs, databases, testing and technical documentation. His AI work focuses on practical workflow design, provider integrations, prompt preparation and human review. These interests come together in DivineGuard’s Gray Fox AI and GovCon Pulse development initiatives.

Building DivineGuard

As the company’s principal engineer, Aaron coordinates requirements, implementation priorities, testing and handoff. He works with the team and specialist providers to define delivery boundaries, identify missing expertise and make responsibilities clear.

Community and workforce development

Aaron’s education and workforce connections include EmployNV, his membership and training at the UNLV Cyber Clinic, and ongoing conversations with CSN and local education programs. DivineGuard welcomes appropriately scoped collaboration with Clark County colleges and workforce organizations.

Education is another part of that direction. Aaron is discussing proposed IT workforce training pathways connected with City of Las Vegas, CSN and Westside Education and Training Center initiatives. The instructor role, program scope and start date remain subject to approval.
