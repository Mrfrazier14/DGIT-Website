About DivineGuard

Our Mission

Technology You Can Trust

DivineGuard IT Services LLC exists to give businesses, nonprofits, and government partners access to enterprise-grade IT support without the enterprise-level complexity. We combine responsive remote support with a security-first mindset, so our clients can focus on their mission instead of worrying about downtime or vulnerabilities.

Whether we're resolving a help desk ticket, hardening a network, building custom software, or supporting a government contract, our goal is the same: deliver reliable technology solutions our clients can depend on.

What Sets Us Apart

  • Clear communication and honest recommendations
  • Experience across commercial and government environments
  • Flexible support models — from one-time projects to ongoing partnerships
