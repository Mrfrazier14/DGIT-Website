# DivineGuard IT Services LLC Website

Static business website for DivineGuard IT Services LLC.

## Pages

- Home
- Services
- About
- Contact

The contact form is configured for Netlify Forms.


## Services and pricing pages
`apps.html` introduces Gray Fox AI and GovCon Pulse as scoped pilot add-ons. `pricing.html` publishes the owner-set $600/month Custom Business AI service; all other services are quoted per scope. Third-party usage, setup, support limits and terms must be set in each proposal. No checkout or automatic subscription is enabled.


Product-line launch: `gray-fox.html` and `govcon-pulse.html` provide consultation offers, onboarding steps and qualified readiness statements. Product CTAs select the matching Netlify inquiry form option. No payment is taken and no customer account is provisioned by form submission.
