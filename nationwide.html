GOVERNMENT BUYERS & PRIME CONTRACTORS

From a small task
to a defined workshare.

Micro-purchase inquiries

Request a quote for a focused technology task, support engagement, software improvement or training session. Provide the scope, delivery location, target date and buyer requirements so we can assess fit and pricing.

The purchasing organization determines the applicable purchasing method and requirements. Payment method and terms are confirmed in the quote.

Larger procurement & subcontracting

We welcome RFQ, RFP and prime-contractor teaming inquiries for documented technology capabilities. Share the public notice or reference, statement of work, response deadline and intended role.

Before a commitment, we review eligibility, capacity, licensing, insurance, required representations, partner responsibilities and customer approvals. An inquiry does not establish an award or contract vehicle.
