How Billing Works

Secure payment options

Stripe payment setup is in progress. We will confirm the options available for your engagement before requesting payment.

Cards & ACH

Stripe-hosted payment pages can offer credit/debit cards and ACH bank payments when enabled. Available methods appear on your checkout or invoice. ACH payments may take time to confirm.

Invoices & Payment Links

For hourly and custom work, approve your quote first. We then send an invoice or payment link for the agreed amount.

Recurring Managed Services

Monthly managed-service billing is arranged after you approve the scope, monthly amount, and service terms. Your plan and payment authorization must be confirmed before recurring charges begin.
